About SASi

The chip design club at the University of Minnesota.

Undergraduates learning, building, and taking designs from RTL to fabrication-ready layouts. A CSE Campus Life Program and a founding chapter of the ASIC Network.

The flow Five stages, RTL to signoff ASIC Network Clubs working in the open The club Advisors and affiliation

The flow

Five stages. Plug in wherever you're curious.

Every stage needs people. Pick the one you like.

  1. /0.1

    RTL Design

    Describe the hardware in SystemVerilog.

  2. /0.2

    Verification

    Prove it does what you claimed it does.

  3. /0.3

    Synthesis

    Map that logic onto real standard cells.

  4. /0.4

    Place & Route

    Floorplan, clock tree, and every wire.

  5. /0.5

    Signoff

    DRC and LVS clean, ready for a shuttle.
