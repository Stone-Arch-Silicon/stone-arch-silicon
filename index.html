// About

We design chips, start to finish.

Almost nobody gets to design a chip as an undergrad. SASi exists so you can.

⌁

The whole flow

RTL to signoff, hands-on, nothing skipped.

⚙

Tools you'll use again

Cadence, Synopsys, and Siemens on our own 20-node cluster.

▣

A path to the fab

Open PDKs and shuttle programs, so clean designs can actually ship.

◈

People who review your work

Teammates who will tell you your timing doesn't close.

// The Flow

Five stages. Plug in wherever you're curious.

Every stage needs people. Pick the one you like.

01

RTL Design

Describe the hardware in SystemVerilog.

→
02

Verification

Prove it does what you claimed it does.

→
03

Synthesis

Map that logic onto real standard cells.

→
04

Place & Route

Floorplan, clock tree, and every wire.

→
05

Signoff

DRC and LVS clean, ready for a shuttle.

// Ways In

You do not need to have taken the class.

Four equivalent ways to qualify. We care whether you can do the work.

Path 01

Coursework

EE 2301 or any equivalent digital logic or HDL course.

Path 02

ASIC 101

Our own curriculum. Start from zero, pass the assessment.

Path 03

Portfolio

HDL, testbenches, FPGA builds, open-source hardware, or research.

Path 04

A conversation

A short review with someone on the board.

Open to every UMN student, any major.
