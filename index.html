The flow

Five stages. Plug in wherever you're curious.

Every stage needs people. Pick the one you like.

  1. /0.1

    RTL Design

    Describe the hardware in SystemVerilog.

  2. /0.2

    Verification

    Prove it does what you claimed it does.

  3. /0.3

    Synthesis

    Map that logic onto real standard cells.

  4. /0.4

    Place & Route

    Floorplan, clock tree, and every wire.

  5. /0.5

    Signoff

    DRC and LVS clean, ready for a shuttle.

Inside the chip

From a few hundred lines of RTL to layout.

Standard cells, metal routing, and a power grid: every layer of a design is something a member writes, checks, or signs off. Drag the package to look around.
