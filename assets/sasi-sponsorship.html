Stone Arch Silicon University of Minnesota

Sponsor SASi

Silicon is expensive. Undergrads are not.

SASi is the chip design club at the University of Minnesota, where undergraduates learn the full ASIC flow from RTL to GDSII on industry EDA tools and open-source PDKs. If you hire people who can do that, this is where they come from.

Where your sponsorship goes

/01

Hackathons

Hands-on chip design hackathons for students at UMN and across the ASIC Network.

/02

The ASIC Network

Shared docs, open analog and digital tutorials, and network-wide events for the university chip design clubs we helped found.

/03

Conferences

Travel and registration so students can present their work at conferences and competitions.

/04

Tapeouts

Shuttle slots and mask costs, so clean designs can actually ship.

/05

Tools & compute

Licenses and cluster hardware for the whole flow.

/06

ASIC 101

Keeping our free curriculum current for every student who starts from zero.
